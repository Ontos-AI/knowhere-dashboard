# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We expect contributors to keep discussion respectful, focused, and constructive. Examples of welcome behavior include:

- using inclusive and professional language
- accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the community and project
- showing empathy toward other contributors

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, abusive comments, doxxing, sexualized language or imagery, sustained disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

To report a conduct concern, contact `team@knowhereto.ai`.
